HOW MOVES WERE TIERED (v3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TIER 4 = OPTIMAL. The moves a competitive player would actually pick, for each type and each category
    (physical / special). Reliable and risky options sit side by side when both are viable:
    Flamethrower AND Fire Blast, Earthquake, Thunderbolt AND Thunder, Ice Beam AND Blizzard, Surf AND Hydro Pump.
    Some roles have their own optimal pick, tagged in the file: [Priority] (Aqua Jet, Mach Punch, Sucker Punch...),
    [Fixed damage] (Seismic Toss, Night Shade), [Defensive] (Body Press, Foul Play).
  TIER 3 = NEAR-OPTIMAL. A move is Tier 3 only if a better Tier 4 option of the same type exists, judged on
    average risk/reward, not raw power. Every Tier 3 entry names that better option ("-> T4: ...").
    Also here: recharge and 2-turn moves (Hyper Beam, Solar Beam, Fly, Dig) and conditional moves (Stored Power).
  TIER 2 = MID GAME. Plain ~50-70 effective-power moves: Flame Wheel, Spark, Wing Attack, Bite, Headbutt.
  TIER 1 = EARLY GAME: Tackle, Ember, Water Gun, plus trivial status moves (Growl, Tail Whip, Harden).

  v3 RULES
    1. PIVOTS ARE TIERED BY RAW POWER. U-turn (70), Volt Switch (70) and Flip Turn (60) are Tier 2 and keep their
       [Pivot] tag, so mid-game trainers can use them. Since no stronger pivot exists, Tier 3 and Tier 4 sets are
       still allowed to pick a Tier 2 [Pivot] move when it is the best option for the Pokémon's role.
    2. VARIABLE / CONDITIONAL MOVES ARE TIERED BY PRACTICAL POWER.
       Always-active boosts stay high: Knock Off (97.5 BP vs. a held item, which almost every Tier 2+ Pokémon has),
       Return / Frustration (102 BP), Facade (140 BP when statused) are Tier 4; Acrobatics (110 BP itemless) is Tier 3.
       Conditions that are almost always met on the right user move up to Tier 3: Payback (100 BP on slow users),
       Avalanche and Revenge (120 BP when hit first), Assurance (120 BP if the target was hit this turn),
       Retaliate (140 BP when an AI trainer sends a Pokémon in after a KO).
    3. NO SNOWBALL, HIGH-RNG OR FIXED-DAMAGE MOVES IN TIER 1. Moved to Tier 2: Rollout, Ice Ball (up to 480 BP),
       Echoed Voice (up to 200 BP), Fury Cutter (up to 160 BP), Rage, Present (can roll 80/120 BP),
       Dragon Rage (40 HP) and Sonic Boom (20 HP).
    4. NO ENTRY HAZARDS. Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web are omitted (listed at the end).
       Stone Axe stays as a plain 65 BP Rock move (Tier 2) without its hazard effect.
       Former hazard removers are judged on what is left: Rapid Spin (50 BP, +1 Spe, frees from Bind/Leech Seed) and
       Mortal Spin (30 BP, 100% poison, same freeing effect) are Tier 2; Defog stays Situational as [Disruption / Field];
       Tidy Up stays Tier 4 [Setup] (+1 Atk, +1 Spe, clears Substitutes).

  STATUS MOVES follow the same rules:
    Tier 4: Spore, Dark Void, Swords Dance, Nasty Plot, Calm Mind, Bulk Up, Dragon Dance, Quiver Dance, Shell Smash...
    Tier 3: Sleep Powder, Lovely Kiss (-> Spore), Coil, Growth.   Tier 2: Hypnosis, Sing, Charm, Screech, Work Up...
    Situational: Toxic, Will-O-Wisp, Protect, recovery, weather, screens..., each with a [Function] label.
  Multi-hit 25 BP moves (Bullet Seed, Icicle Spear, Rock Blast...) stay Tier 3; they play like Tier 4 on Skill Link users.
  Excluded: Z-Moves, Max/G-Max moves, Let's Go-only and CAP moves, doubles-only support moves and entry hazards
    (both listed at the end).
  Kept: moves removed in Gen 8/9 (Return, Hidden Power, Pursuit, Signal Beam...) since this is a custom game.

COLUMNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Move                Cat     BP   Acc  PP Pri  Effect
    Cat: Phys / Spec / Stat    BP: var = variable power, fix = fixed damage    Acc: — = never misses    Pri: priority
    [Exclusive: X line] = only that evolution line in the roster can learn it (signature move).
    -> T4: X = the better Tier 4 option this Tier 3 move is a weaker version of.
