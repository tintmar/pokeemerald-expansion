  biome). Thresholds were seeded from the Gen 1-3 septiles and held fixed while balancing.

LEGEND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Origin:  Base  = Gen 1-3 (#001-#386)
           Line  = Gen 4+ member of a Gen 1-3 evolution line (e.g. Magnezone, Sylveon, Annihilape)
           Added = Gen 4+ Pokémon added only to equalise biome x category counts
  Line:    the evolution family the Pokémon belongs to (families stay in one biome, except branched Eeveelutions
           and Shedinja, which follow their own types)

DESIGN DECISIONS TO VALIDATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Evolution lines: every family is complete (verified by script) and lives in ONE biome, so wild areas show
     whole lines. Exceptions: Eevee (Normal, any biome) with each Eeveelution in its own type biome, and Shedinja
     (Dark) split from Nincada/Ninjask.
  2. Forms: base forms only. Megas, regional variants and evolutions that need a regional pre-evolution are out:
     Obstagoon, Perrserker, Cursola, Sirfetch'd, Mr. Rime, Runerigus, Sneasler, Overqwil, Clodsire.
     Non-regional cross-gen evolutions are in (Kleavor, Annihilape, Farigiraf, Dudunsparce, Wyrdeer, Ursaluna...).
  3. Normal-type rule: Normal Pokémon fill gaps in every biome, capped at 3 per Cat 1-2 cell, 2 per Cat 3-7 cell
     and 9 per biome, so each biome keeps its type identity.
  4. Gen 4+ additions (non-legendary, 29 families): chosen by an optimiser that equalises every
     biome x category cell while adding as few Pokémon as possible, preferring older generations and type/theme fit.
     Paradox Pokémon and Ultra Beasts were not used (legendary-level stats without the Legendary tag).
     Families: Bronzor, Buneary, Carnivine, Cubchoo, Darumaka, Dedenne, Deino, Drifloon, Druddigon, Ferroseed,
     Gible, Gimmighoul, Glameow, Glimmet, Larvesta, Maractus, Pawmi, Pawniard, Riolu, Rookidee,
     Sandile, Sigilyph, Sizzlipede, Snom, Snover, Stunfisk, Togedemaru, Turtwig, Woobat.
  5. Category 9 = 2 per biome. Gen 1-3 has only 6; 23 legendaries exceed 600 BST, which cannot split evenly into 9
     biomes, so 18 were chosen by theme. Left out: Palkia, Koraidon, Miraidon, Solgaleo and Lunala (the last two would
     also force Cosmog/Cosmoem into Cat 8 at BST 200/400). Arceus (Normal) fills Poison, where Eternatus is the only
     on-type option. Any of these can be swapped 1-for-1 within a biome.
  6. Category 8 = 3 per biome: all 15 Gen 1-3 sub-legendaries/mythicals + 12 additions picked to fit biome types
     (e.g. the Swords of Justice, the Loyal Three for Poison, Darkrai/Marshadow/Spectrier for Dark).
